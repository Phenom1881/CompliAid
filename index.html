What is CompliAid

Clarity where it counts most.

FEMA's Public Assistance program is complex, high-stakes, and unforgiving of documentation errors. CompliAid puts compliance knowledge in the hands of the people who need it most, local governments, nonprofits, and recovery organizations working under pressure.

📋

Policy Interpretation

Ask in plain language — “Is force-account labor eligible under Category B?” — and REACT answers from FEMA PA policy, with the basis for the answer rather than a guess.

Ready When You Are
